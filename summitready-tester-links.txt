WHAT TO TEST
--------------------------------

1. GPS Hike Tracking (highest priority)
   - Trails tab → "Start Hiking"
   - Walk outdoors for at least 5–10 minutes, then tap Finish
   - Check: map route accuracy, distance, elevation, background tracking

2. Hill Finder AI
   - Trails tab → "Find Hills with AI"
   - Allow location access
   - Check: hills load near your location, map displays correctly

3. Onboarding & Login
   - Delete and reinstall the app
   - Go through the full sign-up flow
   - Report any crashes, errors, or confusing steps

4. Training Plan & Calendar
   - Open the training plan tab
   - Tap a scheduled session to view details
   - Check for layout issues, overlapping text, missing content

5. Navigation & Crashes
   - Rapidly switch between all tabs
   - Navigate back and forward through screens
   - Report any blank tabs, stuck navigation, or full app crashes

6. Subscription / Premium Screen
   - Settings → Subscription, or tap any premium feature
   - Check the paywall loads correctly with monthly/annual options
   - No real purchase needed — just verify the screen works

7. Visual / Layout Issues
   - Note any cut-off text, off-screen buttons, or broken screens
   - Test on both portrait and landscape if possible

--------------------------------
WHEN REPORTING A BUG, PLEASE INCLUDE:
--------------------------------
- What you were doing when it happened
- What you expected vs. what actually happened
- Your device model and OS version (e.g. iPhone 14, iOS 17.4)
- A screenshot or screen recording if possible
