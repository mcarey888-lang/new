Not release-ready; the package is not fully complete. Core recording, backup, ownership, sharing and profile repairs have been implemented. Community model actually implemented

  • Ownership: personal plans and owned recorded hikes are distinct. A saved member route is a revocable reference, not a copy claiming completion or evidence.
  • Audience: Public means signed-in members; Followers requires the follower relationship; Only me requires ownership. Unknown legacy sharing stays conservative. Audience changes are scoped to the selected recording, not retroactively to every hike.
  • Viewing: backend list/detail reads re-check current access. Owner/private data and cached image state are fenced across account changes; end-to-end switching remains unverified.
  • Following: a member route can be a navigation reference, but progress requires the viewer's new GPS record. Saved plans/reference geometry are never submitted as the viewer's actual trace.
  • Discovery: member lists support search, pagination, nearby starting points, detail maps, references, follow and reports. A combined community-discovery map remains unfinished.
  • Trust: no GPS recording or contribution becomes a canonical/verified trail automatically. Pending trail submission is implemented, not an approval workflow.
