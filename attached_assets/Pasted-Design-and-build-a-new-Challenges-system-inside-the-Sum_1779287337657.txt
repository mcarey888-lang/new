Design and build a new “Challenges” system inside the Summit Ready app.

IMPORTANT:
- Keep the current app design, styling, branding, and navigation consistent.
- Do not redesign the whole app.
- Build this as a new feature/module.
- Keep the first version simple enough to work reliably.
- Focus on motivation, progression, and repeat app usage.
- The system should feel like mountain preparation, not a gimmicky fitness game.

FEATURE NAME:
Summit Ready Challenges

CORE IDEA:
Users complete virtual mountain challenges by logging elevation gain, distance, hikes, or training sessions in their local area.

The purpose is to help users train for big mountains using local hills and everyday terrain.

MAIN CHALLENGE TYPES:

1. Elevation Challenges
Users complete a total elevation target over a chosen time period.

Examples:
- Everest Elevation Challenge: gain 8,848m
- Mont Blanc Challenge: gain 4,809m
- Kilimanjaro Challenge: gain 5,895m
- Ben Nevis 5x Challenge: gain 6,725m total
- Base Camp to Summit Challenge: gain 3,500m

2. Weekly Push Challenges
Shorter challenges designed to build consistency.

Examples:
- 1,000m elevation in 7 days
- 2,500m elevation in 14 days
- 5 hikes in 30 days
- 10,000 steps uphill equivalent challenge

3. Mountain Prep Challenges
Challenges linked to real summit goals.

Examples:
- Mont Blanc Prep Month
- Kilimanjaro Base Builder
- Everest Base Camp Endurance Block
- Alpine Legs Challenge

ACTIVITY LOGGING:

Users should be able to log progress manually for MVP.

Log fields:
- activity name
- date
- elevation gained
- distance
- duration
- notes

Example:
Activity name: Local hill repeats
Elevation: 450m
Distance: 6km
Duration: 1h 30m

After logging, update:
- challenge progress
- weekly totals
- completed milestones
- stats dashboard

Future-proof the system so GPS/Strava/Apple Health/Google Fit integration can be added later, but do not require it for MVP.

PROGRESS SYSTEM:
