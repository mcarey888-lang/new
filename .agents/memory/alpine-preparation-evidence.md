---
name: Alpine preparation evidence
description: User direction for recording technical skills and acclimatisation without treating booked trips as completed.
---

Let users record existing crampon/ice axe skills, training, and acclimatisation manually, including experience gained before using the app. A booked mountain trip may include planned acclimatisation days or technical instruction; that future provision is not yet a completed requirement.

**Why:** The user explicitly said prior skills should be enterable, while many major trips bundle training and acclimatisation. Automatically declaring technical competence from workout totals or a booking would overstate readiness for hazardous terrain.

**How to apply:** Keep planned and completed entries distinct; label completed entries self-reported, not verified. Continue to treat the requirement checklist as preparation guidance rather than safety certification. Affiliate links to courses and guides are a separate future enhancement, not a prerequisite for entering prior experience.
