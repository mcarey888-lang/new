name: Free version product requirement
description: SummitReady uses a free plan alongside optional paid Pro.
---

SummitReady should offer a free plan alongside optional paid Pro.

**Why:** The user stated, "I want to offer a free version of the app," then
explicitly selected "Free plan + optional paid Pro" rather than making the
entire app free.

**How to apply:** Preserve no-subscription access when changing monetization.
Keep Pro access optional and separate. Confirm the scope of free features
before altering paid access; this choice is not permission to make every Pro
feature free or to change the paid subscription prices.
