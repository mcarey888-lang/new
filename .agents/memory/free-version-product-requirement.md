The owner supplied a three-tier brief on 2026-10-26, explicitly superseding
the earlier two-tier briefs. Launch scope is the UK.

**Why:** Explore is for discovery and ordinary recording; Expeditions is for
simulated adventures; Pro prepares users for a real mountain.

**How to apply:**

- Explore is free indefinitely, with unlimited ordinary GPS recordings,
  summit/hill discovery, own activity history, basic stats and general hiking
  achievements. Safety, basic location, recording/recovery and personal-data
  access must not become paid.
- Free gets one explicit, verified starter expedition in full, with replay,
  stages, protected progress mountain and celebration. Never substitute a
  “one active expedition free” rule that unlocks the entire catalogue over time.
  Do not invent starter routes or treat source-only candidates as verified.
- Expeditions includes the full existing expedition collection/progression,
  supported substitutions and celebrations, but no personalised training,
  readiness assessments, adaptive schedules or coaching.
- Pro includes both expeditions and personalised training/readiness/coaching.
  Earlier free initial-readiness and first-week-training previews are superseded.
  Fitness questionnaire and goal setup are free. Save the setup separately and
  let users reach Training Basecamp with a locked readiness meter and programme.
  Offer Pro to unlock these results, not before the questionnaire; do not compute
  personalised free plans/readiness merely to hide them later.
  **Why:** On 2026-10-30 the owner explicitly moved the paywall after the fitness
  profile, with readiness and the programme locked at Training Basecamp.
- Preserve existing full-access subscribers as full Pro, including restoration.
  Expiry retains activities, progress, plans and clearly historical readiness;
  it never interrupts saving a recording. Ordinary hiking on any mountain is
  not participation in a paid simulated expedition. Recording only must also
  stay separate from training progression: explicit linking is required to
  count a hike toward a training session or expedition stage, and a previously
  credited hike must not count again even after a long recording history.
- UK targets are Expeditions £4.99/month or £34.99/year, Pro £9.99/month or
  £69.99/year. The owner will configure store prices. Display actual localised
  store prices, not target-price fallbacks. Pro's desired 14-day trial requires
  a real eligible store offer; Expeditions has no separate trial.
- Readiness estimates training preparedness, not climbing safety, technical
  competence or acclimatisation. Do not promise unlimited AI or unsupported
  navigation, offline maps or device integrations.
- Development changes do not authorise publishing, submitting builds, changing
  live billing, destructive production migrations or resetting user data.

## Starter verification scope

The owner chose **The Lake District Mont Blanc** as the intended free starter,
superseding the suggested Fuji/Australian/Scottish shortlist.

**Why:** The owner explicitly selected this existing expedition on 2026-10-27.

**How to apply:** Focus starter verification on this expedition; selection is
not approval of its route evidence, permission to invent tracks, or permission
to publish. Do not substitute another candidate without asking.

When selecting the free starter, inspect existing curated content and source
approval evidence before proposing replacement geography. Missing verification
metadata does not itself mean the underlying content is wrong.

**Why:** On 2026-10-27 the owner explicitly asked to preserve existing geographic
data, distinguish accuracy gaps from progression and metadata gaps, and complete
only checks supported by real evidence.

**How to apply:** Shortlist three existing candidates with their exact remaining
gaps. Recommend the smallest evidenced follow-up, not a blanket content rewrite.
Do not silently reactivate the wider paused route-database acquisition initiative
or approve a full itinerary based only on a source fragment or an approved image.
