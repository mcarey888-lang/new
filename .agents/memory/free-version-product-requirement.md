SummitReady should offer a free plan alongside optional paid Pro.

**Why:** The user stated, "I want to offer a free version of the app," then
explicitly selected "Free plan + optional paid Pro" rather than making the
entire app free.

**How to apply:** Preserve no-subscription access when changing monetization.
Keep Pro access optional and separate. Follow the supplied feature policy
below; the tier choice does not authorize changing paid subscription prices.

## Free/Pro feature policy

Free helps people explore, record hikes and discover whether they are ready.
Pro actively prepares them for a mountain.

**Why:** The user supplied a detailed Free/Pro brief with this distinction,
intending useful free access to build trust, activity data and the community
route library rather than making Free a deliberately poor app.

**How to apply:**

- Free mountain/hill browsing is unrestricted, including photography, facts,
  overviews, publicly available mapped routes, route statistics, verification
  status, basic 2D maps, parking/trailheads, hazards, access and weather links,
  and reporting incorrect or dangerous information.
- Free recording is unlimited in count and duration. Include background and
  offline GPS, breadcrumbs, distance/time/ascent, pause/resume/finish,
  photographs, mountain association, recovery, personal history and sync when
  connectivity returns. Do not hide historic activities.
- Free routes include online basic following, GPX import, own-activity GPX
  export, basic elevation/difficulty, three saved routes, and one on-demand
  mapped-route lookup/build every 30 days. GPS evidence contribution requires
  consent and anonymisation. Ordinary map browsing is not route generation.
- Free readiness includes a full initial assessment, score, strengths/gaps,
  preparation period, target-mountain comparison and one reassessment every
  30 days. Pro adds continuous updates, history, forecasts and advanced
  breakdowns.
- Free training includes one active summit goal, plan overview, first-week
  or first-three-session preview, manual completion, basic weekly/elevation
  targets, general preparation guidance and a basic checklist. Pro supplies
  the complete adaptive programme and multiple-mountain preparation.
- Free expeditions include one complete starter and one active expedition,
  local-hill contribution, animated Mountain Progress, stage markers, a
  basic map and standard completion. Do not remove the protected Mountain
  Progress experience from Free. Pro adds the destination library,
  customisation, advanced progress/celebration and unlimited expeditions.
- Pro route features include unlimited saved routes, fair-use route
  generation, full planning, advanced overlays/3D/flyover and advanced
  navigation/analysis. Apply internal anti-abuse fair-use limits even to Pro.
- Offline GPS recording is Free. Prepared offline route/map/navigation
  packages are Pro only when working and permitted by provider licensing.
  This tier policy does not itself authorize enabling map downloads.
- Free accounts include privacy controls, personal-data export, purchase
  restoration, basic achievements and no advertising.
- Never paywall safety/emergency/access warnings, basic hazards,
  parking/trailhead information, recording, basic GPS location, ending or
  recovering an activity, personal history/data export, reporting dangerous
  or incorrect information, or route verification status.
- Device integrations and live/emergency sharing must not be advertised
  before they work reliably. Do not describe unavailable capabilities as
  unlocked merely because the customer has Pro.
- Upgrade prompts should follow meaningful intent: after an assessment or
  plan preview, another expedition, an available offline download,
  additional route generation, or advanced progress. Do not constantly
  interrupt ordinary exploration.
