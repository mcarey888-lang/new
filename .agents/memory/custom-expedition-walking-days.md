---
name: Custom expedition walking-day capacity
description: User-defined stage availability, independent of consecutive calendar days.
---

Custom expeditions must allow users to enter their own positive whole-number walking-day allowance, rather than restricting them to one, two or three days. Stages can be completed over weeks or months; the allowance is not a requirement to walk on consecutive dates.

**Why:** The user explicitly said expeditions may take weeks or months and requested removal of the three-day restriction.

**How to apply:** Keep initial creation and later customisation consistent. Treat the allowance as capacity, not a reason to invent extra summits or empty stages. Preserve real target ascent, distance, route identities and provenance. Keep curated expedition facts separate from a custom availability override.

Larger availability windows must not cause unbounded combinatorial searches. Finish comparing a cardinality before choosing its best normal match, stop once the minimum normal cardinality is established, and disclose any computation-budget interruption rather than claiming exhaustive optimisation.

**Why:** Removing the old day restriction allows exponentially more route combinations; calendar flexibility must not exhaust the shared API or manufacture confidence in a partially searched result.

**How to apply:** Preserve the normal/widened matching priority and existing small-plan results when changing planner performance limits.
