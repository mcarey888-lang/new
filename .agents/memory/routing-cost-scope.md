---
name: Routing cost scope
description: Early-stage routing budget and provider-selection constraint.
---

Prefer low-cost routing while SummitReady has few paying users. A paid hosted hiking provider remains an option when paying usage grows, not an already approved subscription.

**Why:** On 2026-10-24, after seeing hosted hiking prices, the owner said they would like to keep costs down for now and could use that service once the app has lots of paying users.

**How to apply:** Assess free-tier limits, commercial-use permission, attribution, caching rights and reliability before proposing a provider. Do not purchase or commit to a recurring paid plan based on the earlier provisional provider choice.
