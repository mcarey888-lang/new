Incline Treadmill uses the right-half crop of its own treadmill library image on the session card, but uses the Stepper library image on session detail. This mismatch was explicitly presented to the user, who approved it before asking for the same cropped-card and selected-exercise approach across the other exercises.

**Why:** The approved pairing is not a filename mistake to silently correct; the user confirmed it after being asked specifically about the Stepper hero for Incline Treadmill.

**How to apply:** Keep that one session-detail exception when refining exercise imagery. Other exercise cards use their respective library artwork, and their detail views should follow the selected exercise. Ask before replacing the approved exception with a treadmill image.

Exercise artwork on session detail should use the full exercise-library image as a full-width, top-edge hero without outer padding, not a thumbnail or a lettering-free crop behind the title. The mission card image should be a narrow, full-height strip flush with the card's left edge, not a floating square.

**Why:** The full-bleed art under a strong scrim looked mostly black, while the lettering-free crop hid the actual artwork. The user identified the intact exercise image previously shown lower in “Where and when” as the exact one wanted in the hero, and asked to remove that section. A subsequent screenshot showed the image inset below the header; the user explicitly wanted it flush to the page's top and side edges. The source artwork itself does not need resizing.

**How to apply:** Keep mountain photos as backgrounds where appropriate. Render the full exercise art first, edge-to-edge and at its natural proportions, then the screen header and session details below so controls do not obscure its lettering. On the mission card, keep the image narrow but stretch it to the card's full height with no left inset; preserve the text inset and Start action. Preserve the selected exercise identity and the approved treadmill/stepper exception. If reorganizing this screen, keep hill, exercise and day controls accessible even though the “Where and when” section is gone.
