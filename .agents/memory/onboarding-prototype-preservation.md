---
name: Onboarding prototype preservation
description: Preserve SummitReady onboarding design work across later iterations and cleanup.
---

Keep each new onboarding workflow as a dedicated, clearly named prototype with its source and canvas preview intact. Create a checkpoint before replacing, integrating, or removing it, and do not delete an earlier approved direction without explicit confirmation.

**Why:** A previous Training/Expeditions onboarding demo was removed during cleanup and could no longer be reopened from its original live preview.

**How to apply:** Build future onboarding work in a dedicated prototype location, retain versioned directions during iteration, and only graduate a copy into the production app after approval.
