---
name: Local hill imagery privacy
description: Product trust rules for generic local hill imagery and owner-chosen journey photos.
---

Generic artwork for local training hills must be visibly labelled as illustrative rather than passed off as a photo of a named hill. A journey photo may become that person's private hill cover only after an explicit choice; do not turn it into shared hill imagery automatically.

**Why:** Obscure hills often have no trustworthy matching web photo. Journal pictures can depict people, private locations, or a different hill, and their owner has not consented to public reuse.

**How to apply:** Keep the illustrative default available offline, match a personal cover to an exact hill identity and owner, and fall back when its source photo is removed. A public photo submission would require separate consent, identity checks, privacy treatment and review.
