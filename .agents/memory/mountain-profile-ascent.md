---
name: Mountain profile ascent semantics
description: Defines how target-mountain elevation gain should be measured for Virtual Expeditions.
---

Target-mountain elevation gain means uphill ascent on the standard route from the conventional trailhead, lift station, or tram terminus normally used by climbers. It must not be calculated from the nearest valley town or inferred from summit altitude.

**Why:** Mont Blanc was inflated to roughly 3,800m by measuring from Les Houches. The normal Goûter route begins at Nid d’Aigle and is approximately 2,400m of ascent; inflated profiles distort local route selection and match scoring.

**How to apply:** Clarify this definition in profile-generation prompts, keep verified overrides for well-known routes, and version cached profiles whenever ascent semantics change.
