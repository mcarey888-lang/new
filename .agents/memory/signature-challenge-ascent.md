---
name: Signature challenge ascent meaning
description: Semantic boundary between a fixed local expedition challenge and its mountain-route inspiration.
---

Signature expeditions are fixed local-stage challenges. Their target ascent and distance come from the sum of their curated UK stages. A mountain route used for inspiration is a separate comparison; do not show a mountain-route ascent as the challenge target unless its start, lift/transport choice, route and reliable source are stated. If any of these are unknown, withhold the mountain-route figure rather than inventing one. Do not silently retarget an already saved expedition.

**Why:** Matterhorn's Zermatt-to-summit figure was displayed as an unqualified expedition ascent while the UK stages totalled much less. Climbers often start at Schwarzsee; both route figures can be legitimate for different starts, but mixing either with local-stage progress damages trust.

**How to apply:** When curating new challenges or editing old ones, keep local challenge progress distinct from the named mountain route, surface the source/assumptions for any route comparison, and offer an explicit migration choice before changing an existing user's saved target.
