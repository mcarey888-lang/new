---
name: Saved sample profiles
description: How to present an account-owned fictional activity year without falsifying verified evidence or publishing stories.
---

The user chose a saved sample year rather than a temporary preview. Treat it as an account-owned, clearly labelled fictional layer that can be enabled or removed independently of real activity. Its avatar and photos are illustrative, not personal photographs; stories remain private.

**Why:** The existing development preset can replace local training history, while the Elevation Bank and challenge evidence represent actual recorded work. Creating ordinary activity or community rows for fictional achievements would misrepresent both progress and authorship.

**How to apply:** Keep the sample layer separate from canonical activity, verified credits, rank evidence, and public member feeds. Do not use destructive development preset loaders on a signed-in account. Preserve the user's real data and make sample figures explicitly distinguishable on every screen where shown.
