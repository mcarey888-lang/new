name: You tab hero consistency
description: User-approved shared hero across Feed and the other You tabs.
---

Keep the same cover image, profile identity, and metrics at the top of Feed, Profile, Achievements, Photos, and Stats. The content beneath the tab bar changes; the hero does not switch to a compact or text-only header on Feed.

**Why:** The user explicitly corrected a Feed-first redesign that replaced the profile hero with a small “You” header. They want the Feed entry point to remain visually consistent with the other You tabs.

**How to apply:** Any future feed or tab reorganization should preserve the approved shared profile presentation above the tab bar unless the user explicitly requests another header treatment.

Keep Feed content immediately below the tabs compact: the “Share a moment” bar comes first, without a separate “Mountain stories” introduction. Retain the post list and its audience switcher.

**Why:** The user wants community posts to appear on the initial screen without scrolling, while keeping the shared hero intact.

**How to apply:** Avoid adding feed-only headings, descriptions, or large spacers between the You tabs, composer, and first posts.

Do not restore the inline “Edit profile” and “Discover member routes” links
between the shared hero and the tabs. Use white, dark-text feed cards within
the dark app shell to highlight posts.

**Why:** The owner explicitly removed those links to reduce wasted space and
requested the light feed treatment shown in their reference. Editing remains
available through existing profile/settings controls.

**How to apply:** Keep the tabs close to the metrics on every You section.
Maintain the light treatment for real and explicitly fictional preview cards,
but keep sample reactions noninteractive and separate from real member counts.
