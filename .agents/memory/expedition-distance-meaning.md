---
name: Expedition distance meaning
description: Manual versus GPS distance semantics for expedition completion statistics.
---

Manual expedition completions use the mountain stage's listed full walking-route
distance once per completed repetition. Circular and out-and-back route totals
must not be doubled. GPS completions retain actual recorded kilometres, even
when those differ from the planned route.

**Why:** The owner found two listed stages of 12 km and 13 km displaying 50 km
on completion and explicitly requested 25 km for manual logging, versus actual
tracked distance for GPS completions.

**How to apply:** Keep manual save summaries, activity evidence, progress credit
and completion/share totals consistent. Prefer explicit walking distance over
distance away. Do not overwrite GPS evidence with catalogue distances or silently
rewrite historical activity records.
