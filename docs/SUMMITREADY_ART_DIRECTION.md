# SummitReady Art Direction

**Gate:** MV-C03

## Brand frame

SummitReady imagery should feel observed on a real mountain day: credible
terrain, changing weather, practical equipment and controlled natural colour.
The premium character comes from authenticity, composition and restraint, not
fantasy scale or synthetic gloss.

## Global rules

- Prefer outdoor editorial/cinematic photography with believable geography.
- Preserve atmospheric depth and natural dawn, dusk or golden-hour light.
- Compose for dark UI overlays with deliberate negative space.
- Store focal point and safe area with the master.
- Do not bake in UI text or logos unless typography is the asset itself.
- Reject impossible routes, fantasy peaks, excessive saturation, fake
  technical gear, duplicated anatomy and implausible climbing scenes.
- People should use context-appropriate clothing, packs, poles and technique.

## Family direction

| Family | Direction |
|---|---|
| Mountain | Recognisable terrain and weather; objective scale; summit is not always centred |
| Expedition | Journey and remoteness; coherent visual identity across stages; people secondary to place |
| Training | Real local hills, stairs, treadmills and strength work; practical form, no commercial-gym gloss |
| Route / DNA | Terrain texture, ridges, paths and navigational clarity; avoid implying false route geometry |
| Rank / Achievement | Abstracted terrain, contour, altitude and material cues; no invented evidence or military insignia |
| Challenge | One clear objective with identifiable place/activity; derivatives share the master identity |
| Onboarding / UI | Immediate product purpose, accessible subject placement and generous copy-safe area |
| Marketing / store | Strong single promise, real product framing and crops designed for store constraints |

## Master and derivative targets

Targets follow current mobile hero/card, admin review and marketing usage:

| Use | Master | Primary derivatives |
|---|---:|---:|
| Mobile structural hero | 2400×3000 (4:5) | 1170×1140 near-square hero; 1200×800 card; 600×600 thumbnail |
| Route/DNA landscape | 2400×1600 (3:2) | 1200×800; 800×600; 600×600 |
| Achievement/Rank graphic | 2048×2048 | 1024×1024; 512×512; 256×256 |
| Marketing landscape | 2560×1440 (16:9) | 1920×1080; 1200×630; store-specific crops |
| Store portrait | 2400×3000 (4:5) | device/store crops produced from safe-area metadata |

Do not upscale weak sources to meet a nominal master size. Imported assets keep
their original dimensions and receive explicit quality/placement limits.

## Prompt structure

Prompts should name subject identity, geography, season/weather, viewpoint,
time/light, human activity/equipment, composition/focal placement, negative
space, colour restraint, realism and placement intent. Negative instructions
must cover text/logos, fantasy terrain, impossible exposure, anatomy, gear,
oversaturation and generic AI sheen.
